    Spotlight listings scoring
    Brands rank by their best deal score where a listing shows its RRP, otherwise by index score. Spotlight outlines listings at or above the score, here and as more load. Sorting moves the best-scoring listings to the top of the grid (Vinted). A filled tick means the brand still has a listing showing; brands whose listings are all hidden (by the filter, or by the site's own filters) are greyed out. Click a brand to jump to its listings.

    Scan pages from here
    Collects every listing from this search, page after page, into one list. There you can filter by score, price, size and condition, compare brands, find steals (priced well below that brand's going rate in the scan), and save listings. By default it reads the pages in the background. Tick the box to have it click the page buttons in this tab instead; it does this anyway if the background read fails. Leave the tab open while it scans.
